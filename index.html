Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Locoquiz offers short personality and self-reflection quizzes. This policy explains what information may be handled when you use the site, including information used to display and measure advertising.

Your quiz answers

Your answers and scores are processed in your browser. Locoquiz does not ask you to create an account, and it does not save your quiz answers or results on its own servers.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, hosting, security, analytics, or advertising providers may automatically receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, approximate location, pages viewed, referring page, and the date and time of your visit. This information may be used to deliver the site, protect it from abuse, understand general traffic, and measure advertising.

Advertising, cookies, and similar technology

Locoquiz uses Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, or similar identifiers to:

  • show personalized or non-personalized ads;
  • limit how often an ad appears;
  • measure ad performance and site activity; and
  • detect fraud and protect advertising systems.

Your choices and the ads you receive may depend on your location, consent settings, browser controls, and Google account settings. Blocking cookies may affect some site or advertising features.

Third-party services

Google AdSense, the site’s hosting provider, and other service providers process information under their own privacy policies. Locoquiz does not control how those independent providers use information they collect. Advertisements may also link to third-party websites, whose privacy practices are their own.

Data retention

Locoquiz does not retain your quiz answers or score after they are processed in your browser. Technical and advertising information may be retained by the providers that collect it according to their own retention policies.

Children’s privacy

Locoquiz is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The site does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Your choices

You can manage or block cookies through your browser and review advertising choices through your Google account. You may also leave the site at any time without submitting quiz answers.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site’s features or service providers change. The effective date at the top will show when the latest version took effect.
